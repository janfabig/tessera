How to open Tessera
===================

Tessera keeps windows you tiled next to each other together. Arrange a few
windows edge to edge, for example with Rectangle. When you later switch to one
of them (with AltTab, Cmd-Tab, the Dock or a click), Tessera brings the others
forward too, and the window you picked stays on top. Move one of the windows
away by hand and the group is gone.

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later.
Nothing else has to be installed. Tessera works in the background: it has no
window and no Dock icon, only a small icon of nine squares in the menu bar.


1. Install it

   Drag Tessera.app into your Applications folder.


2. Open it the first time

   Tessera is not notarised by Apple, so macOS blocks it once.

   - Double-click Tessera. macOS says it cannot check the app. Close the
     message.
   - Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

   Or do it in one step: open the Terminal app, paste this line and press
   Return (change the path if you put Tessera in a subfolder). After that
   Tessera opens like any other app.

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Tessera.app


3. Give it Accessibility access

   Tessera needs it to see and raise windows. When you open it, macOS asks.
   Click "Open System Settings" and turn on Tessera under Privacy & Security
   -> Accessibility. Tessera starts working right away.

   If you closed that message, open the page yourself: System Settings ->
   Privacy & Security -> Accessibility, click +, pick Tessera and turn it on.

   Tessera also adds itself to your Login Items, so it starts again whenever
   you log in. To stop that, untick "Start at Login" in its menu.


4. Use it

   - Tile two or more windows next to each other, for example left and right
     half. They form a group as soon as you place them.
   - Switch to any of them: the others come forward with it.
   - Drag one of them away: it leaves the group.
   - Groups are per display. Tessera forgets them when it quits.


5. The menu

   Click the nine squares in the menu bar.

   - Pause Grouping: switching raises only the window you pick, until you
     untick it. The icon is greyed out meanwhile.
   - Start at Login: whether Tessera starts when you log in.
   - Hide Menu Bar Icon: Tessera keeps working without the icon. To get it
     back, open Tessera.app again (for example from your Applications
     folder or with Spotlight).
   - Quit Tessera.


6. Updates

   Quit Tessera and replace Tessera.app in your Applications folder with the
   new one. Do step 2 again. macOS then treats it as a new app, so in
   Privacy & Security -> Accessibility select the old Tessera entry, remove
   it with the minus button and open Tessera to be asked again.


Privacy

   Tessera only looks at the position and order of your windows. It sends
   nothing anywhere and has no network access.
